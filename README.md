# JsMusic

Is the offline music player that allows You to play music downloaded on Your Android phone.


# Features

— Don't need the Internet connection to work
— Intuitively understandable interface
— 13 color themes (6 themes White-COLOR, 6 themes Black-COLOR, Black-n-White theme)
— Creating and managing playlists (For example, you can change Your playlist's pic by a photo from Your Gallery. (By default, it's the automatic created pic from first 4 song's pics))
— Creating and changing notes (comments) to songs (up to 500 characters!)
— Searching songs in Your Library by name, author, note (comment)
— Full-screen and mini players
— You can see song's cover in Full-screen player
— Player works even while it's minimized or phone screen locked
